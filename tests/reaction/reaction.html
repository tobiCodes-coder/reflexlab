How to play

  1. Press Start (or click the area / press SPACE).
  2. Wait while the area is red. Do not click yet.
  3. When it turns green, click / tap / press your key as fast as you can.
  4. Clicking during red = False Start (round invalid).

Tips for consistent results

  • Use the same device and input method each time.
  • Rest your finger near the button before the round.
  • Take several attempts — your average matters more than one score.
  • Avoid testing when tired or distracted.

What is a good reaction time?

On a visual reaction test like this one, the average person scores around 250–300 ms. Casual gamers usually land between 200–250 ms, and experienced competitive players often react in 150–200 ms. Consistently under 200 ms is considered very fast.

Reaction time shifts with sleep, focus, age, caffeine and device latency — so judge your average across several attempts, not a single round. Full breakdown: What is the average reaction time?
