How to play

  1. Choose a test type (time or word count) and a text category.
  2. Click the input box and start typing the passage.
  3. The timer starts on your first keypress.
  4. Correct characters turn green-ish, mistakes turn red. Backspace fixes mistakes.
  5. Finish the text or let the timer run out to see your result.

How the score is calculated

  • WPM = (correct characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes elapsed. One "word" = 5 characters.
  • CPM = correct characters ÷ minutes elapsed.
  • Accuracy = (keystrokes − errors) ÷ keystrokes × 100.
  • Consistency = how steady your per-second speed was (not the same as accuracy).
  • Corrected mistakes still count in the error total — fixing them only improves accuracy slightly.

Tips

  • Accuracy first, speed second — speed follows clean typing.
  • Keep your eyes on the passage, not the keyboard.
  • Short daily practice beats occasional long sessions.
