What is an aim trainer?

An aim trainer isolates the mechanical mouse skills used in games and precision work: how fast and how accurately you can move a pointer to a target, stay on it, and switch between targets. It trains hand-eye coordination — not game knowledge or decision-making.

The skills

  • Precision — clicking targets carefully; accuracy first.
  • Flick — fast movement to a distant target.
  • Micro-Flick — tiny, controlled adjustments.
  • Switching — moving quickly between several targets.
  • Tracking — keeping the pointer on a moving target (press and hold).
  • Reaction — responding the instant a target appears.
  • Speed — rapid target acquisition under time pressure.

How scoring works

  • Score = hits × 100 (+ streak bonus).
  • Accuracy = hits ÷ (hits + misses). Misses always count — ignoring targets never helps.
  • Acquisition time = target appears → target hit.
  • Consistency = how repeatable your acquisition times were (not absolute skill).
  • Time on target (tracking) = % of session your pointer stayed on the target.

Practice tips

  • Short daily sessions (10–15 min) beat rare long ones.
  • Accuracy first — speed comes for free later.
  • Use the same mouse and sensitivity every session.
  • Track your average, not your single best run.
