How to play

  1. Choose a duration (1–30 seconds or custom).
  2. Press Start — the timer begins on your first click.
  3. Click / tap / press your key as fast as you can until time runs out.
  4. See your CPS (Clicks Per Second), total clicks and category.

Tips for a better score

  • Rest your finger on the button before starting.
  • Try different techniques: regular, jitter or butterfly clicking.
  • Short bursts (1–5s) give higher CPS; long tests measure endurance.
  • Keep your hand relaxed — tension slows you down.
