What Is the Average Reaction Time?

ReflexLab Guides · 3 min read

Quick answer: The average human reaction time to a visual stimulus is about 250–300 milliseconds (ms). Sound is faster (~170 ms) and touch is fastest (~150 ms).

What is reaction time?

Reaction time is the gap between a stimulus appearing and your body responding to it. When a light turns green and you click, your eyes detect it, your brain processes it, and your hand moves. The total time of this chain is your reaction time.

Average reaction time by stimulus

  • Touch: ~150 ms
  • Sound: ~170 ms
  • Sight: ~250 ms

This is why sprinters react to a starting pistol, and why most online reaction tests (including ours) use visual cues around the 250 ms average.

Average reaction time by age

  • Teens (14–17): ~250 ms
  • 20s: ~230–250 ms (peak performance)
  • 30s: ~250–270 ms
  • 40s: ~270–300 ms
  • 60+: ~300+ ms

Reaction time peaks in your early 20s and slowly declines after that — but regular training and good sleep can keep you sharp at any age.

Gamers vs non-gamers

Studies and large online datasets consistently show gamers reacting faster than non-gamers. Casual players average around 230–250 ms, while professional esports players often score between 150–200 ms. Fast-paced games like FPS titles train exactly this skill.

How to improve your reaction time

  • Sleep 7–9 hours — tired brains react slower
  • Practice daily with a reaction test (consistency beats intensity)
  • Exercise regularly — blood flow speeds up neural processing
  • Reduce screen latency (wired mouse, high refresh-rate display)
