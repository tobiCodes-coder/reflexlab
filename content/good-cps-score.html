What Is a Good CPS Score?

ReflexLab Guides · 3 min read

Quick answer: The average person clicks at 6–7 CPS (clicks per second). A score of 8+ is good, and 10+ is pro gamer level.

What is CPS?

CPS stands for clicks per second — how many times you can click a mouse button in one second. It is a popular measure of finger speed, widely used in gaming communities and clicking challenges.

CPS score levels

  • Below 5 CPS: Turtle pace — just warming up
  • 6–7 CPS: Average human
  • 8–9 CPS: Fast — better than most people
  • 10–12 CPS: Pro gamer territory
  • 12+ CPS: Elite clicker (usually with special techniques)

Test duration matters

CPS drops the longer the test runs. Most people can burst 9–10 CPS for 1 second, but only hold 6–7 CPS over 10 seconds. That is why serious players compare scores using the same test length — 5 seconds is the most popular standard.

How do people click so fast?

  • Normal clicking: one finger, steady pace (6–8 CPS)
  • Jitter click: tensing the arm to vibrate the finger (8–12 CPS)
  • Butterfly click: two fingers alternating (10–14 CPS)
  • Drag click: dragging the finger across the button (15+ CPS)

Warning: jitter and butterfly clicking strain your wrist. Take breaks — speed is never worth an injury.

How to increase your CPS

  • Practice short daily sessions (2–3 minutes, not hours)
  • Use a light gaming mouse with a responsive switch
  • Keep your wrist relaxed and your grip stable
  • Track your personal best and beat it slowly
