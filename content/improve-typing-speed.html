What Is a Good Typing Speed?

ReflexLab Guides · 3 min read

Quick answer: The average typing speed is 40 WPM (words per minute). 60+ WPM is good, and professional typists reach 80–120 WPM.

What is WPM?

WPM means words per minute — the standard measure of typing speed, where one "word" counts as 5 characters including spaces. It is used by employers, typing tests, and typing games worldwide.

Average typing speed by group

  • Casual users: 25–40 WPM
  • Students: 30–50 WPM
  • Office professionals: 50–80 WPM
  • Professional typists / programmers: 80–120 WPM
  • World-class competitors: 150+ WPM

What is a "good" typing speed for YOU?

It depends on your goal. For everyday chatting, 40 WPM is fine. Most data-entry and admin job listings ask for 60–70 WPM. If you write code or articles for a living, 80+ WPM saves you hours every week.

Accuracy first, speed second

Typing 80 WPM with mistakes is slower than 60 WPM clean — every error costs a backspace and a break in focus. Professionals aim for 97%+ accuracy, and speed follows naturally.

How to improve your typing speed

  • Learn touch typing — fingers on the home row (ASDF JKL;)
  • Never look down at the keyboard
  • Practice 15 minutes daily instead of hours once a week
  • Fix your posture: feet flat, wrists floating, screen at eye level
  • Test yourself weekly and track your personal best
